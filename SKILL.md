# VIP Onboarding

  This is an OfficeX Playbook. Agents can save this as a plan prompt.

Welcome to OfficeX! This VIP onboarding playbook walks new trial members through 6 milestones designed to show you every part of the platform. Complete all milestones and pass the quiz to earn between 50 and 200 bonus credits.

## The 6 Milestones

1. **Redeem Your Voucher** — Enter your trial voucher code on the onboarding page to activate your account and receive your starting credits.

2. **Run the Core Marketing Playbook** — Pick one of the core marketing playbooks (Reddit Narrative Seeding, X One-Shot Funnel, FB Group Builder, or Mass Viral TikToks) and run through at least the first 2 steps. This shows you how playbooks work end-to-end.

3. **Try a Content Creation App** — Install and use a content creation app like X Authority Builder or Saucebot Bookmarks. Generate or curate at least one piece of content.

4. **Try a Lead Gen App** — Install and use a lead gen app like Reddit Lead Gen, X Lead Gen, or Related Subreddits. Run at least one search to see how lead sourcing works.

5. **Try a Utility App** — Install and use a utility app like Dollar Platoon or Linkpop Email. Set up at least one gig inbox or create one email address.

6. **Complete the Onboarding Quiz** — Take the quiz to test what you learned. Score 50% or higher to earn bonus credits:
   - 50-69%: 50 bonus credits
   - 70-89%: 100 bonus credits
   - 90-100%: 200 bonus credits

## Tips

- For each milestone, you can either run the app yourself or watch the tutorial video. Both count.
- You don't have to complete milestones in order, but we recommend following the sequence.
- After completing the quiz, you'll receive an email with a voucher code for your bonus credits. Redeem it on the same onboarding page.
